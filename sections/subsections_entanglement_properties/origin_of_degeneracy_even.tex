\subsection{Origin of Degeneracy}\label{sec:origin_of_degeneracy}

\valert{
The block-diagonal structure of $\rho_A$ implies a massive degeneracy in the entanglement spectrum, since all the blocks in the density matrix are identical. In this section, we interpret a direct correspondence between the gauge part of the entanglement and the degeneracy in the entanglement spectrum. We also explain the emergence of the matter part of the entanglement.
}

Based on the block-diagonal structure \eqref{equ.rho-block-diagonal-structure} of $\rho_A$ and the fact that $\hat{\zeta}$-operators can relate different blocks, explained by \eqref{equ.related-blocks}, we then conclude that there are 
\[2^{L-1} = 2^{\abs{\de A}/2 - 1}\] 
identical blocks in $\rho_A$. 
%The reason why we only have the number $2^{L-1}$ instead of $2^L$, which is the number of blocks in \eqref{equ.rho-block-diagonal-structure}, is that $\hat{\zeta}$-operators can only relate blocks with the same parity of the set of quantum numbers $\lrg{\xi}$, so the number of blocks, $2^L$, is actually twice the number of identical blocks. 
The factor $2^{-1}$ arises from the fixed parity of quantum numbers, which can also be understood a consequence of the exact 1-form Wilson symmetry~\cite{Xu-Rakovszky-Knap-Pollmann_2025}. After tracing out the environment $\overline{A}$, the global Wilson operator in the subregion $A$ becomes a string with two endpoints attached to the boundary of $A$, and commutes with $\rho_A$ at the same time. The two endpoints of the Wilson operator can flip the residual $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge field at the boundary, playing a role in relating different blocks of $\rho_A$. This is the same as the $\hat{\zeta}$-operators. Based on the block-diagonal structure of $\rho_A$, it can be expressed as
\begin{equation}\label{equ.block-rho-final}
    \rho_A = \bigoplus_{n=1}^{2^{L-1}}\varrho^{(n)}_A
\end{equation}
with all the blocks $\varrho_A^{(n)}$ identical. Substitution of such an expression into the definition of von Neumann entropy directly gives the entanglement entropy, 
\begin{align}\label{equ.EE-from-blocks}
    S_A 
    &= -\Tr_A\rho_A\ln\rho_A \nonumber \\
    &= -\Tr_A\lrm{\bigoplus_{n=1}^{2^{L-1}}\lr{\varrho^{(n)}_A \ln \varrho^{(n)}_A}} \nonumber \\
    &= -2^{L-1}\Tr_A\varrho_A \ln \varrho_A \nonumber \\
    &= -\Tr_A\Tilde{\rho}_A\ln\Tilde{\rho}_A + \lr{\frac{1}{2}\abs{\de A} -  1}\ln 2
\end{align}
where $\Tilde{\rho}_A \equiv 2^{L-1}\varrho_A$ is the normalized density matrix
defined to make $\Tr_A\Tilde{\rho}_A=1$. By comparing Eq.~\eqref{equ.EE-from-blocks} with Eq.~\eqref{equ.EE-separation}, we find that each block $\Tilde{\rho}_A$ corresponds to the Majorana reduced density matrix $\rho_A^F$. At the same time, the gauge part $S^G_A$ measures the number of identical blocks in $\rho_A$, which is exactly the degree of degeneracy in the entanglement spectrum. This agrees with the gauge nature of such a contribution since the degeneracy 
%arises from the block-diagonal structure of $\rho_A$ along with the fact that every block is identical, and the presence of block-diagonal structure is due to the residual electric field from the 
is yielded by the residual Gauss law after the environment is traced out. Finally, the topological entanglement entropy $-\ln 2$ is a consequence of the topological constraint that the total parity of the quantum numbers, $\prod_{n=1}^L\xi_n$, is fixed, and it originates from the exact 1-form Wilson symmetry. 
%The most important thing is that the full entanglement spectrum of the Kitaev ground state is obtained. Since an entanglement spectrum is defined as the spectrum of a reduced density matrix, formula \eqref{equ.block-rho-final} directly answers that the entanglement spectrum is the eigenvalue spectrum of $\varrho_A$ with $2^{\abs{\de A}/2-1}$-fold degeneracy. As shown by the comparison between \eqref{equ.EE-from-blocks} and \eqref{equ.EE-separation}, we know that the block $\varrho_A$ is proportional to the Majorana fermion density matrix $\rho^F_A$, and whose spectrum can be solved exactly using the method proposed in Ref.~\cite{Jin-Korepin_2004, Vidal-Latorre-Rico-Kitaev_2003, Latorre-Rico-Vidal_2004, Peschel_2003, Peschel-Eisler_2009, Chung-Peschel_2001, Cheong-Henley_2004}.

From a more fundamental point of view, the set of $\hat{\xi}$-operators block-diagonalizes every string operator in a way identical to what we have for $\rho_A$ because each of them commutes with every string operator, hence so is $\mathcal{S}^{(A)}_{\mathfrak{D}}$
\[
[\hat{\xi}_n,\mathcal{S}^{(A)}_{\mathfrak{D}}]=0 \;\forall n\in\lrg{1,2,\cdots,L}
\]
Similar to equation \eqref{equ.block-rho-final}, every $\mathcal{S}$-operator can also be block-diagonalized as
\begin{equation}\label{equ.U-V-block-relation}
    \mathcal{S}^{(A)}_{\mathfrak{D}} = \bigoplus_{n=1}^{2^{L-1}}\mathcal{M}^{(n)}_{\mathfrak{D}}
\end{equation}
where $\{\mathcal{M}^{(n)}_\mathfrak{D}\}$ is a set of identical blocks of $\mathcal{S}^{(A)}_{\mathfrak{D}}$.

To ensure that expression \eqref{equ.U-V-block-relation} satisfies rule \eqref{equ.U-rep-algebra}, we also have to implement such an algebraic rule on every block $\mathcal{M}^{(n)}_{\mathfrak{D}}$, 
\[
\mathcal{M}^{(n)}_{\mathfrak{D}}\mathcal{M}^{(n)}_{\mathfrak{D}'} = \varphi(\mathfrak{D},\mathfrak{D}')\mathcal{M}^{(n)}_{\mathfrak{D}\ominus\mathfrak{D}'}
\]
%This is reminiscent of \eqref{equ.V-rep-algebra}, which shows that the $\mathcal{M}$-representation is identical to $\mathcal{S}$. 
Based on this, we have confirmed that those blocks $\mathcal{M}^{(n)}_{\mathfrak{D}}$ in \eqref{equ.U-V-block-relation} are precisely $\mathcal{M}$-operators defined in \eqref{equ.V-rep-def}. In short, the gauge-field degree of freedom is actually the number of identical blocks of string operators represented by spins, and each identical block depicts the physics of free Majorana fermions. This precisely reflects the fractionalization of the spin degree of freedom into gauge-field and matter sectors, with the matter sector further fractionalizing into deconfined Majorana fermions.
